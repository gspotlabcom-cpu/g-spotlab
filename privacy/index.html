Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026
Website: g-spotlab.com
Publisher: G-Spot Lab

1. About this website

G-Spot Lab is an independent publisher of shader toolkits and ready-to-use VFX assets for Unity and Unreal Engine. This website presents our products, demonstrations and links to external marketplaces.

There are no visitor accounts, registration forms or checkout on g-spotlab.com. Purchases and downloads take place on Unity Asset Store or Fab.

2. Information we handle

We handle only the information needed to operate and understand the website:

  • Basic technical information needed to deliver the website through GitHub Pages.
  • Optional analytics information if you choose to accept analytics cookies.
  • Email information if you contact us directly.

We do not sell personal information and do not collect payment card details through this website.

3. Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is disabled when you first visit the website. It loads only after you select Accept in the analytics notice.

If accepted, analytics may record pages viewed, referring sources, approximate location, device and browser details, scrolling, video opens, visits to the toolkit catalog, marketplace clicks and use of the support-email copy button. We use this information to understand which products and content are useful to visitors.

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies to distinguish visits and sessions. Google states that Analytics uses IP addresses during collection but does not log or store individual IP addresses. Our setup keeps advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization disabled.

If you select Reject, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded by this website. You can review or change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the website footer. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website saves your analytics choice in your browser's local storage so it does not need to ask again on every page. This preference is not used to identify you.

If analytics is accepted, Google Analytics may use first-party analytics cookies. If analytics is rejected, the website keeps Analytics disabled and removes its analytics cookies where the browser permits. You can also manage cookies and site data through your browser settings.

5. YouTube demonstrations

Product preview images are loaded from YouTube's image servers. This means your browser connects to Google when those previews are displayed and sends the technical information normally included in a web request.

A YouTube player is loaded only after you choose to open a demonstration. Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode through youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube and Google may still process information under their own terms and privacy policy.

6. Hosting and external services

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to Unity Asset Store, Fab, YouTube and other external websites take you to services operated by those providers. Their own privacy policies and terms apply after you leave g-spotlab.com.

7. Email and support

If you email G-Spot Lab, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and may keep the correspondence for support, business records or legal obligations.

8. Your choices and questions

You can reject optional analytics or change your choice through Cookie settings. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may also have rights relating to personal information you have provided directly to G-Spot Lab.

For privacy questions or requests, contact gspotlab.com@gmail.com.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when the website or the services it uses change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
