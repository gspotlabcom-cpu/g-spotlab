Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026
Website: g-spotlab.com
Publisher: G-Spot Lab

1. About this website

G-Spot Lab is an independent publisher of shader toolkits and ready-to-use VFX assets for Unity and Unreal Engine. This website provides product information, demonstrations and links to third-party marketplaces. Purchases and downloads take place on those marketplaces, not through this website.

This policy describes how personal information may be handled when you visit g-spotlab.com, interact with embedded content or contact G-Spot Lab.

2. Information handled by the website

G-Spot Lab does not provide visitor accounts, registration forms or an on-site checkout. We have not installed our own advertising trackers or website analytics on this site.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you access it, the hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver the site, maintain security and operate its services. For information on GitHub's practices, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. YouTube previews and videos

Some product previews are loaded from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com). Your browser may connect to Google or YouTube when those images are loaded, and the provider may receive technical information about the request.

Video playback is initiated when you choose to open a demonstration. The website then loads a player from youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedding domain). That domain does not guarantee that YouTube processes no information or uses no browser storage. YouTube's own terms and privacy practices apply to its services. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Marketplace and other external links

Our website links to Unity Asset Store, Fab, YouTube and other third-party services. Following such a link takes you to a service operated by another provider. Their privacy policies and terms govern information you provide or activity on their sites. G-Spot Lab does not collect your payment card details through this website.

5. Cookies and browser storage

G-Spot Lab has not intentionally added its own tracking cookies or analytics cookies to this website. External content providers, especially YouTube when video content is loaded, may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. You can manage cookies and site data using your browser settings.

6. Email and support

If you contact G-Spot Lab by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any information you choose to send. We use these details to read and respond to your inquiry and, where necessary, to follow up on support or business correspondence. Emails may be retained as reasonably needed for those purposes or for applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

7. Your questions and privacy rights

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information handled by G-Spot Lab. For inquiries about information you have sent us directly, contact us using the address below. Information processed independently by GitHub, Google, Unity or Epic Games must also be addressed through those providers where appropriate.

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the website, its integrations or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest published revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact gspotlab.com@gmail.com.
